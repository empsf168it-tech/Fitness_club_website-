Before you ask.

Is there a joining fee?

No. You pay the monthly rate and that is it. No onboarding charge, no locker deposit, no hidden admin cost.

Can I try before I commit?

Yes. Book a single trial session through the Visit page. No obligation, no sales conversation afterwards unless you want one.

What if I miss a session?

Nothing happens. You are an adult. We track progress, not attendance guilt. Coached members can bank and rebook sessions within the same month.

Do I need experience?

You need to be coachable. That is the only prerequisite. We have trained first-time lifters and former national competitors on the same floor on the same morning.

Do you have showers and lockers?

Yes. Full shower facilities, towels provided, and secure lockers on site. Everything you need to train hard and leave clean.
